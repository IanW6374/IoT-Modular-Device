# IoT-MD v3.0.0-alpha.39

Release sequence: 2744. Native ABI: 6. MicroPython: 1.29.0. ESP-IDF: 5.5.5.

Alpha 39 makes the four viable Upgrade methods the desktop layout authority.
Automatic, Manual, Staged and Rollback therefore retain equal quarter-width
choices as availability changes, rather than stretching two visible choices to
fill the row. The layout becomes two columns on medium displays and one column
on small displays.

Restart is a manual decision rather than measurable transfer work. The final
progress step no longer displays a percentage. Once a signed release or retained
application is ready, that step contains the **Restart and install** or
**Restart and rollback** button itself. Labels remain concise and never append
the artifact type or version.

Alpha 39 includes the Alpha 38 startup-order correction and retains native ABI
6, controlled qualification workflows and the cross-release evidence campaign.
